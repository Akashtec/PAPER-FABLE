A FABLE FOR THE CURIOUS

The Seed That Remembered Spring

✳

Inside a book no one borrowed, you found a paper seed folded into a line of forgotten verse. It was no bigger than a comma, but it felt warm, as if it had been waiting for your hand.

Outside, the first rain wrote tiny footnotes on the leaves. A fox with ink-dark paws appeared beside you, carrying the quiet certainty of someone who knew the way home—and a few roads beyond it.

Together, you followed a path to a garden where forgotten promises had grown into little glowing flowers. You planted the seed and left without asking what it would become. Some things grow best when you let them surprise you.
